Our houses

Two ways to stay between the city and the Cilento

They are not copies of the same hotel. They are different houses, with different spaces and rhythms. What they share is the way they welcome you: rooms kept as you would keep your own, honest advice and a genuine wish to make you feel at home.

01

Salerno · 3 rooms

Portarotese

In the heart of Salerno, a short walk from the seafront and the old town. Three bright rooms — Porta del Mare, Portarotese and Portanova — with private bathroom, air conditioning and, in two of them, a balcony for the evening air. The right base for exploring the city on foot.

  • Private bathroom
  • Balcony
  • Air conditioning
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Flat-screen TV
  • Tea and coffee set

Santa Maria di Castellabate · Cilento

Leucosia
